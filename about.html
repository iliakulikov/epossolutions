About EPOS Solutions

We specialise in the supply, installation, onboarding and long-term support of EPOS systems for Irish retail, hospitality and service businesses.

With over 27 years of experience, our local team builds solutions that are practical on the floor, easy for staff to use and reliable under pressure.

We support both modern cloud-first deployments and legacy EPOS environments, helping businesses upgrade at the right pace while keeping daily operations running smoothly.

Need a tailored setup? Contact us from the homepage and we will recommend the best package for your workflow, location and budget.
